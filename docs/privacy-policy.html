Privacy Policy

ChargeSound · Android
Last updated: 13 October 2026

English  |  한국어

In short: ChargeSound has no accounts, no servers, and no developer-side data collection. Your settings stay on your device. The only data that leaves your device is the standard advertising identifier handled by Google AdMob, which Google uses to serve ads.

1. Who we are

ChargeSound (“the App”) is published by an independent developer. Contact us at hiroobabo0@gmail.com.

2. Data the developer collects

None. The developer operates no account system, no backend server, and no analytics for the App. Nothing you choose or configure is transmitted to us.

3. Data stored on your device

The App stores the following locally using Android’s private app storage. This data never leaves your device:

WhatWhy
Sound and animation preferences (selected preset, volume, vibration, theme)Remember your settings
Which premium items you have unlocked, and your ad-view progressRemember unlock status on this device
The file name and local path of an audio or video file you chosePlay the file you selected
Permission state (notifications, display over other apps)Know whether to show prompts

Clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling the App permanently deletes all of it. The App requests the READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO access only when you personally pick a file through the system file chooser, and the App reads only the file you select.

4. Advertising

The App displays ads from Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Ads are served by Google and its advertising partners.

Data involved in ad serving

  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) — a resettable identifier for your device used to serve and measure ads.
  • Device and ad interaction information — device model, OS version, app version, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and whether an ad was viewed or clicked.

Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Ads policies. We never receive personally identifiable information from Google, and we never ask Google for the names, email addresses, or phone numbers of our users.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID — Android Settings › Privacy › Ads, or reset it at myadid.google.com.
  • Turn off ad personalization — Android Settings › Privacy › Ads › Delete advertising ID, or in-app at Google Settings › Ads on your device.
  • In-app ad-free time — you can watch a rewarded ad to hide banners for a period of your choosing. Declining is always allowed and never blocks any feature.

6. Children

ChargeSound is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the App serves ads, it is not intended for children. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us and we will investigate.

7. Security

All App data stays in Android’s private app storage, which the operating system isolates from other apps. We do not transmit your data, so there is nothing for us to store or protect on our side. Advertising data is transmitted by Google over encrypted connections.

8. Data deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete. You can erase all App data at any time:

  • Android Settings › Apps › ChargeSound › Storage › Clear data, or
  • Uninstall the App.

To delete your Advertising ID, see section 4.

9. Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are in the EEA, UK, or another region with similar laws, you have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, and object to processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

Because we operate no servers and hold no personal data, we cannot fulfill an access or erasure request — there is nothing in our possession. For data held by Google as your advertising provider, use the links in section 4, or Google’s data rights page.
